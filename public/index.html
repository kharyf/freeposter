New campaign

Turn on Autorun for ?

Whichever AI agent is connected to FreePoster may then change this campaign however it thinks best. Every change is listed under Changes with its reason, and you can undo each one.

It may:

  • change the campaign's table: its videos, how many are made, their days, times, lengths and editing styles
  • change the types its videos carry
  • add hooks and angles to the library (for this campaign only)

It may not:

  • change CTA links or add CTAs
  • use a CTA this campaign doesn't already use
  • let a type post without approval, or add a type that posts without approval
  • start or stop the campaign
  • stop or resume campaigns
  • turn Autorun on or off
